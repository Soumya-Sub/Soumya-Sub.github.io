About

01

I'm a Senior Business Consultant based in Gurgaon, working at the intersection of strategy, technology, and execution. I specialise in turning complex, ambiguous problems into clear strategies, compelling business cases, and executable plans across digital infrastructure, airport technology, financial services, enterprise SaaS, communications, retail, hospitality, and real estate.

My work spans market and competitive intelligence, solution and commercial strategy, RFP and proposal development, business-case modelling, solution design, and C-suite stakeholder engagement. I particularly enjoy problems where there isn't an obvious answer — bringing together commercial, operational, and technology perspectives to understand what is viable, where the value lies, and what it takes to move an idea from concept to implementation.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of technology and business economics: not simply what can be built, but why it should be built, how it creates value, and how that value can be translated into a business case that earns investment and drives action. I believe strategy is only as good as the decision it enables, which is why I care equally about the numbers, the narrative, and the outcome — making complex ideas simple enough to understand, rigorous enough to defend, and compelling enough to act on.

Outside work, I volunteer as a tutor at a village school near my hometown and help coordinate community food drives. These experiences have reinforced something I value in both professional and personal life: good ideas matter, but their real value lies in the difference they make. I'm also a landscape photographer and enjoy travelling, particularly to places that offer a different perspective on how people, communities, and cities live and evolve. Both pursuits keep me curious, grounded, and attentive to perspectives beyond my immediate environment.
